FAQ

Buying Coorg Coffee Beans

Answers to common questions from roasters, cafés and buyers looking for Robusta and Arabica coffee beans from Coorg, India.

What is Coorg coffee?

Coorg (Kodagu) in Karnataka is one of India’s best-known coffee-growing regions, where coffee grows under native forest shade in the Western Ghats. Our estate in Palangala, Coorg sits at 1,094m above sea level and borders protected forest, giving dense, slow-matured coffee cherries.

Do you grow Robusta or Arabica coffee beans?

Both. 60% of our estate is planted with Arabica, and we also grow high-grade Fine Robusta at 1,000m+, with clean, high-density beans for commercial and specialty roasters.

How are your coffee beans processed?

Our coffee is 100% natural and sun-dried on the estate for 6 to 8 days, with zero added flavourings, artificial additives or chemical processing aids.
